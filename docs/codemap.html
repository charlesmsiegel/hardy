hardy as a model of its problem

Hardy is an evidence-producing theorem-proving harness in which models propose, Lean checks, and the harness preserves what each artifact actually establishes. Its local layered design separates mathematical content, experimental measurements, runtime control, and human-readable claims.

Chosen interpretation. A proof chatbot could optimize for fluent completion and trust its own transcript; Hardy instead keeps explicit authority and identity boundaries even when that preserves partial or refused work. src/hardy/app/wiring.py:44
