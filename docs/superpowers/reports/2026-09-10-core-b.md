## Implemented boundary

- B0 stores exact record history and context activation in serialized atomic
  transactions under a project `ledger/`, separately from session history.
- B1 derives dependencies, reverse impact, blockers, contexts, research histories
  and transport paths without promoting recorded status to evidence.
- B2 authenticates exact evidence, admission and decision identities through
  named capability-owner operations. Missing operations deny acceptance.
- B4 records scoped representation assessments, source searches, model/configuration
  identity, explicit assumptions and outstanding materialization work.
- B5 manages immutable semantic context branches, notation and transport children,
  preserving original statements and required dependencies in A2 projections.
- B3 derives research, context, trust, coverage, staleness and publication views.
  Open citations and revised unresolved prerequisites remain publication blockers.

## Design retained for review

Exact immutable records describe mathematical history; acceptance is derived from
the independently owned evidence those records name. The event store reuses
`WriteGuard` and `FileLock`; graph and views share one snapshot rather than separate
stores for contexts, research or publication. Replay and indexes cost O(history),
and simple-path enumeration can be exponential. Future workflow adapters attach
named operations at these existing seams. The most sensitive boundary is exact
subject/context/scope/transport binding in capability readers; default denial is
deliberate until those bindings can be authenticated.
