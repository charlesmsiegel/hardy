## What the fixtures establish

G0 composes the actual C2 resolver and source library over a cyclic A/B/C fixture.
Depth 0 leaves direct checks alone; depths 1/2 retain source identities and expose
cycles, missing sources, unsupported/unmapped text, and file/byte/node cutoffs.
Named expansion is attributed, bounded, and unable to overwrite semantic work or
write acceptance. Direct and recursive receipts are authenticated at final
reporting; an external receipt can disappear without a ledger revision.

G1 separates exact textual correspondence from semantic version reading. A moved
unchanged statement retains its mathematical identity and accepted evidence.
Changed hypotheses, notation, deletions and uncertainty reopen current affected
work and its reverse dependents only in the requested exact scope. Historic
acceptance remains authentic history. New statements are not automatically adopted.
Affected admitted premises require a deliberate scope change; the audit refuses
to imply revocation while admission still permits the premise.

Independent composition review caught a missing manuscript-use association:
C2 contracts name their required external claim, while Referee's application
identity was only encoded in the work ID. Referee now persists the exact
manuscript item, source span and work link. Version auditing follows those work
histories to exact contracts and owned child obligations. A two-application C2
fixture proves that changing one use leaves the other intact. Ambiguous or orphaned
legacy mappings require Referee reinventory rather than guessed invalidation.

G2 uses exact CONTAINS relations inside the existing planner, outside mathematical
dependencies. Historical editions, shared prerequisites, hidden helpers, cycles,
visibility, stale prose and minimal per-theorem contexts are exercised. Review
caught flattened nested headings; regression cases now cover nested sections,
following parent-level material and nesting deeper than LaTeX's heading levels.

G3 performs one explicit text-only refresh with exact old/new mathematical refs.
One revision-checked transaction revises the selected prose and DOCUMENTS link,
records model/configuration provenance and drops the old text's evidence. Other
paragraphs, mathematical items and history remain unchanged. Concurrent edits
refuse the whole append. Ordinary mathematical revision invokes no prose callback.

These tests use attributed scripted semantic/capability operations. They establish
software behavior, not mathematical reading completeness, model improvement,
whole-paper correctness, real TeX rendering or process isolation. Application
reader adapters remain explicit configuration; Core E UI/trials are deferred.
