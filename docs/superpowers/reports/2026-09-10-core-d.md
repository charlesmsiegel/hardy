## Acceptance and design limits

D0 selects Mathlib, local definition, local proof and literature routes, then
checks Main modulo exactly the one approved external source. It runs actual
FinalVerifier/C5 over deterministic Lean process responses. A fresh workflow and
ledger reopen with zero attempts and the same trust boundary. Changing Main,
its lemma, definition, downloaded source or source reading revokes reuse. The
real A3 policy records unavailable probe caveats before explicit scripted scope
approval; these fixtures do not claim successful live admission probes.

Research uses C4 for statement preparation and prerequisites before the target
operation. Completion of a computation/refutation inspection does not prove the
target. Exact request history and capability authentication control restart reuse.

Explore fixtures exercise concepts, adequate and stronger representations,
semantic X/f/p declarations, compactness as a local child context, minimal binder
export, weaker-context return, questions/conjectures/goals, blocked approaches,
aliases, chosen witnesses, explicit transport and historical counterexamples.

Critique records typed gaps without repairs. Referee inventories actual source
text, binds claims/citations to exact spans, detects explicit hypothesis/notation/
representation/transport drift, and reports missing or unreviewed source coverage.
Its semantic readings remain explicit inputs, not inferred mathematical certainty.

Repair uses an existing guarded-save owner through a named operation. That owner
retains staging, audit and artifact checks. Reverse dependency rechecks are
observations; only independently authenticated B2 evidence accepts the repaired
obligation. A crash, refusal or overlap leaves open work and preserved history.

Publication plans retain minimal local context and exact prose linkage. Assembly
uses recorded text and the existing document compiler in a fresh output directory;
human edits are not overwritten. Missing artifact-only prose is reported. A
compiled draft does not certify its mathematics.

Model, Lean and policy-owner judgments in these hermetic fixtures are scripted;
ledger/source/artifact IO and shared orchestration are real. Applications still
configure named model, capability-reader and guarded-save operations. Terminal
wiring and all Core E trials are deferred for human input. No real-paper,
live-model, sandbox or benchmark claim follows from these tests.
