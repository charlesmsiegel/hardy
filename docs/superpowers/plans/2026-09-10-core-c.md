## Theory to preserve

The graph stores mathematical history, not verdicts. Acquisition narrows a missing prerequisite into explicit work; it cannot manufacture authority by changing its category. A strategy proposes Lean under run-owned ceilings; the verifier judges the exact final claim. Missing source/hypothesis/transport or unproved holes must survive as visible work after interruption and restart.
