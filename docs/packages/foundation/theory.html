Judge 3

Theory: Reusable infrastructure owns bounded child processes, directory-identity checks, whole-file replacement, operating-system locks, and strict transport-neutral values. These operations establish specific mechanical guarantees for callers without deciding mathematical policy or pretending to provide execution isolation.

Instead of: Let every capability implement its own file checks, mutex heuristics, truncation, and subprocess teardown; shared ownership makes those guarantees consistent and inspectable.
